I still remember exactly how this all started. What began with us just talking in the club turned into something that completely took over my heart.

But the truth is, I didn't need to win a game to prove my feelings. My love was always right there, shining right in front of you, even when you started looking at someone else.

I thought if I just loved you enough, if I was always there, you would eventually see it. I held onto those memories every single day. Every time I brought gifts back from my trips for you, every late night I stayed up to help you with your work, every time I stood by you—I did it because I loved you deeply.

But it broke something in me every time I saw you prioritize someone else. The times you dropped my calls, the times you made me feel invisible while you gave your attention to a man who couldn't even give you a real future. I sat there, helping you through your tears over him, while swallowing my own.

You asked if we could just be good friends. But I cannot be a friend to someone I loved this deeply. I refuse to sit on the sidelines, pretending I am okay, just to be your emotional sponge when things go wrong.

Holding onto a beautiful memory isn't enough when the reality is breaking my heart. I cannot keep fighting for a connection where I am just an option, waiting in the dark for you to choose me.

Those memories on the pahhadi, that first birthday wish, and that first kiss will always be special to me, but I have to leave them behind now. You made your choice, and now I have to choose my own peace.

I really did love you, Srushti.

Goodbye.
