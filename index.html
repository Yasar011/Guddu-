why I call you that

Almost everything I remember about you happened at night.

The walks after dark. Sitting in that Kerala hotel till they were basically waiting for us to leave. Navaratri lights. The night outs. You falling asleep on my lap while everyone else was still awake.

That's the whole reason. You're the thing that showed up when it got dark and made it easier to be there. I never needed you to be the sun. The moon doesn't burn anything — it just shows up, quietly, and the night stops feeling so long.

So that's what I call you. My moon.
